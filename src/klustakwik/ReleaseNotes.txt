KlustaKwik Release Notes

Version 2.0 (modernisation fork)

C++17 port of v1.7. Algorithmic additions: farthest-point seeding,
two-phase CEM with time-merge pass, three-phase temporal chunking for
long recordings with electrode drift, OpenMP parallel chunk processing,
optional CUDA backend. All output formats unchanged. See CHANGES.md for
full details.

Version 1.7 24 Jun 04

Fixed line endings in release archive. Modified building.

Version 1.6 15 Nov 03

Added model saving and penalty mix between AIC and BIC. Internally began factoring of clustering engine from executable.

Version 1.5 21 Mar 03

First release at sourceforge. Included cluster splitting.
